## Final prompts

Each collection image used this prompt with the corresponding subject below:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square luxury jewelry catalog photograph for the Mancini & Co website. Subject: [subject]. Style: photorealistic fine jewelry studio macro product photography. Composition: complete jewelry piece centered, occupying about 65% of a square frame with generous clean space on all sides. Backdrop: completely plain warm ivory matte surface (#f0ece5), soft subtle contact shadow. Lighting: refined diffuse studio light, realistic metal reflections, controlled diamond sparkle, warm champagne mood. Material quality: accurate fine prongs, polished precious metal, precise gemstone facets. No text, logos, watermark, border, human hands, props, boxes, or unrelated jewelry.

- Aurora: A single 18K yellow-gold six-prong solitaire ring with a luminous 2-carat round brilliant white diamond, delicate 2mm band. Three-quarter view, ring upright and slightly tilted.
- Verde: A single 18K rose-gold ring with an emerald-cut deep Colombian-green emerald center stone and a delicate diamond halo, slim diamond pavé band. Three-quarter view, ring upright and slightly tilted.
- Luna: A single pear-cut white diamond pendant on a delicate 18K white-gold chain arranged in a graceful U shape. The pendant sits at the bottom center of the U, chain tips extend toward the upper corners.
- Celeste: A pair of matching 18K white-gold drop earrings with pale blue pear-cut aquamarines hanging below delicate diamond pavé arcs. Neatly aligned together, no ear or human model.
- Serena: An 18K yellow-gold tennis bracelet set with a continuous line of round brilliant white diamonds and a neat clasp, arranged as an open oval, three-quarter top view.
- Notte: An exceptional platinum and yellow-gold diamond collar necklace, articulated line of sparkling diamonds in a U shape, with a large deep midnight-blue sapphire center drop surrounded by a refined diamond halo.
